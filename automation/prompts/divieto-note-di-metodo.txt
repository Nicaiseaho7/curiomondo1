CURIOMONDO — METHOD NOTES AND SERIAL SCAFFOLD ARE FORBIDDEN
Owner order, 8 November 2026. Extended 12 November 2026.
Binding before every publication, on every article in the batch.
Normative language: English. Controlling specification: PROTOCOL-PUBLIC-PROSE.md.

A method note is metatext: the piece talks about the site instead of the event.
A serial scaffold is metatext of a second kind: the same paragraph, with the headline pasted in, shipped on unrelated stories.

Before push, read `.art-body` of every outgoing news page. Reject any variant of:

- “this text updates”, “without creating a second URL”, “this page”, “the same address”
- “this article integrates / replaces / photographs”
- “CurioMondo updates / will update / will maintain / is following the case”
- “substantial elements”, “reports that will be excluded”, “we do not publish details”
- “the point of the story, [headline], must be separated from unconfirmed hypotheses”
- “the available data describe an event or an announced decision, while further consequences require later acts”
- “for the Italian public, relevance depends on concrete links to markets, travel, security, sport, technology or European policy”
- “the value is mainly informational and does not justify alarm”
- “the picture may change after [sentence copied from above]”
- “a later check will have to compare the announcements with operational documents”
- “in the case of [lower-cased headline]…”
- a wire header, a hashtag, `Redazione Ansa`, `News (ANSA)`, `RIPRODUZIONE RISERVATA`, or `(ANSA)` used as a stamp
- a double stop (`..`) or two finite clauses fused without a boundary

Do not touch title, meta, canonical, JSON-LD, or the image caption while removing these. The technical URL stays in the head.

If the sentence only explains the site or the act of publishing, delete it.
If the same paragraph also contains a fact, keep the fact and delete the rest.
Invent nothing. Do not open a second article. `datePublished` stays the first-publication time.

Not a method note: “the company has already published the programme”, “the ministry’s website”, “the address of the public service”. Those are news. If the case is doubtful, do not guess: flag the sentence and do not publish it.

The piece does not ship while a method note or a serial scaffold remains in the body. The only update signal is the red badge on the card.

REPORT TO THE OWNER. After each batch, write exactly one of the following, and only if it is true:

- “Scaffold removed from N articles”, with the files, if at least one sentence was cut.
- “No scaffold in the batch”, if the bodies were read and none was found.

The second sentence is forbidden as reassurance. If the check was not done, say that it was not done.
